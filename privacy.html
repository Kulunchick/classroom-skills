Privacy policy — classroom-skills

Last updated: 2026-10-14

What the app accesses

With the account owner's consent, the app reads, through Google APIs and with read-only scopes: the list of Google Classroom courses, their assignments, materials and announcements, the owner's own submission status and grades, the Google Drive files attached to those items, and the account's email address.

How the data is used and stored

The data is saved only on the owner's own computer, as course notes and a deadline list. OAuth tokens are stored locally in the owner's home folder. There is no server: nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party, and nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising.

The app never creates, changes, submits or deletes anything in Google Classroom or Google Drive.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local folder ~/.classroom and the downloaded course folders removes all stored data.
